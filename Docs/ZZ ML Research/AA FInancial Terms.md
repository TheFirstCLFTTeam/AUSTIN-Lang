# List of financial terms

## Preamble 

This is a list of publicly available financial terms related with payments, loans and structured products that you may consider in your project.

## Payments

Core Concepts:
Payment – Transfer of funds from payer to beneficiary
Funds Transfer – Generic term for moving money between accounts
Clearing – Exchange of payment instructions between institutions
Settlement – Final transfer of funds (and sometimes securities)
Finality – Point at which a payment is irrevocable
Value Date – Date funds become available to the beneficiary
Cut-off Time – Deadline after which processing moves to the next cycle
Intraday Liquidity – Funds available during the business day

Credit Transfer – Payer pushes funds to beneficiary
Debit Transfer – Payee pulls funds (e.g. direct debit)
High-Value Payments (HVP) – Time-critical, large amounts
Low-Value Payments (LVP) – Batch-processed retail-style payments
Cross-Border Payment – Payment across jurisdictions/currencies
Domestic Payment – Payment within a single country
FX Payment – Payment involving currency conversion

Correspondent Bank – Bank providing services to another bank
Nostro Account – “Our money with you”
Vostro Account – “Your money with us”
Intermediary Bank – Bank used in the payment chain
Loro Account – Third party’s account with another bank
Cover Payment – Payment split between messaging and settlement paths

FX Loan – Loan denominated in a foreign currency
Back-to-Back Loan – Loan collateralized by deposits with the same bank

## Structured Products

### What Structured Products Are

Structured Product / Structured Note – Instrument combining a derivative with a bond or note
Payoff Profile – How returns depend on the underlying
Underlying Asset – Equity, index, rate, FX, commodity, or basket
Capital Protection – Partial or full principal protection
Capital at Risk – Principal can be lost
Issuer – Bank issuing the note
Senior / Unsecured Debt – Credit ranking of the note
Issuer Credit Risk – Exposure to the issuing bank

### Common Product Types

Autocallable – Early redemption if conditions are met
Reverse Convertible – High coupon, downside equity risk
Capital-Protected Note
Yield Enhancement Product
Participation Note – Tracks upside of an underlying
Digital / Binary Note – Fixed payoff if condition met
Range Accrual Note
Phoenix / Athena Note
Worst-of Basket Product
Callable Note – Issuer has right to redeem
